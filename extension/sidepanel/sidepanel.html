Legal Notice, Mission & Disclaimers

Why This Project Exists

Indiana's Second Chance Law looks great on paper, but the vocabulary we use around it is doing enormous, unhelpful work. The state feels empathy for people with records, so it wrote a statute. But empathy is just a feeling; actual care is resource allocation. Right now, the state's empathy is locked behind a gate with up to a $3,000 admission fee, operated by private attorneys. It is an economic filter that locks out the exact people who need a clean slate the most.

I built this tool because that filter is a structural choice, not an inevitability. You shouldn't have to buy your way back into a system you already paid for. This software is not legal counsel. It is not absolution. It is the raw infrastructure required to do the work yourself — the same work an attorney charges thousands to format on your behalf. The law belongs to you. This is just the machinery to use it.

Crucial One-Shot Rule Warning (IC § 35-38-9-9(i))

Under Indiana Code § 35-38-9-9(i): “A person may file a petition for expungement of a conviction record under this chapter only one (1) time during any period of the person's life.”

IF YOU OMIT ANY CONVICTION, YOU CAN NEVER EXPUNGE IT LATER. All petitions across different counties must also be filed within 365 days (IC § 35-38-9-9(d)).
