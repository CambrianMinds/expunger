# Indiana Expungement Court Filing & Procedural Guide (IC § 35-38-9)

A comprehensive procedural manual for filing petitions for expungement of arrest records and criminal convictions in the 92 counties of the State of Indiana.

---

## 1. Statutory Framework Under Indiana Code § 35-38-9

Indiana's "Second Chance Law" (IC § 35-38-9) establishes distinct statutory tiers for expungement eligibility based on the offense category and disposition:

| Statute Section | Category | Waiting Period | Standard of Relief | Court Fee / Waiver |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **IC § 35-38-9-1** | Arrests, Charges Dismissed, Acquittals, Infractions | 1 year from date of arrest/charge | **Mandatory** (Court *shall* grant if requirements met) | No filing fee required |
| **IC § 35-38-9-2** | Misdemeanors & Minor Class D Felonies reduced to Class A Misdemeanors | 5 years from date of conviction | **Mandatory** (Court *shall* grant if requirements met) | Standard civil filing fee applies; eligible for fee waiver |
| **IC § 35-38-9-3** | Class D & Level 6 Non-Violent Felonies | 8 years from date of conviction | **Mandatory** (Court *shall* grant if requirements met) | Standard civil filing fee applies; eligible for fee waiver |
| **IC § 35-38-9-4** | Major Non-Violent Felonies (Class A, B, C; Levels 1–5) | 8 years from conviction or 3 years from sentence completion | **Discretionary** (Court *may* grant after weighing equities) | Standard civil filing fee applies; eligible for fee waiver |
| **IC § 35-38-9-5** | Felonies Resulting in Serious Bodily Injury / Official Misconduct | 10 years from conviction or 5 years from sentence completion | **Discretionary + Prosecutor Consent** required | Standard civil filing fee applies; eligible for fee waiver |

## 2. Critical Legal Safe-Harbors (Mandatory Knowledge)

### The Lifetime One-Shot Rule (IC § 35-38-9-9(i))
> *"A person may file a petition for expungement of a conviction record under this chapter only one (1) time during any period of the person's life."*

**Permanent Forfeiture Warning:** If you have convictions in multiple counties or multiple offenses within one county, **ALL convictions you ever wish to expunge must be filed in your lifetime petition packet**. If you intentionally or accidentally omit any conviction from your petition, that omitted conviction **can NEVER be expunged for the rest of your life**.

### The 365-Day Multi-County Filing Window (IC § 35-38-9-9(d))
If you have convictions across different Indiana counties (e.g., a misdemeanor in Marion County and a felony in Allen County):
- You must file separate petitions in each county's Circuit or Superior Court having criminal jurisdiction.
- **All petitions across all counties must be filed within a single 365-day period** commencing on the date the first petition is filed.
- Once 365 days elapse, your lifetime opportunity to petition any remaining counties is permanently exhausted.

## 3. Standard Pleading Packet Structure (Forms 00–08)

The document generator compiles a standardized 10-pleading petition packet adhering strictly to **Indiana Trial Rule 10**:
- 8.5 × 11 in dimension with 1.0-inch margins on all sides.
- Times New Roman / Georgia serif typography with 12 pt minimum font size.
- Formal Trial Rule 10 case captions with county name, judicial circuit, cause number, and Trial Rule 7(A) designation.

### Packet Pleadings Checklist:
1. **Form 00:** Petitioner Intake & Pre-Filing Statutory Certification (Verification checklist).
2. **Form 01:** Verified Petition for Expungement of Arrest & Conviction Records (IC § 35-38-9).
3. **Form 02:** Petitioner's Affidavit of Specific Character & Conduct.
4. **Form 03:** Chronological Case Summary (CCS) Docket Attachments & Cause Schedule.
5. **Form 04:** Certificate of Service upon County Prosecutor, County Sheriff, ISP, and BMV.
6. **Form 05:** Verified Motion to Waive Prepayment of Court Filing Fees and Court Costs (Indigency Application).
7. **Form 06:** Petitioner's Financial Affidavit Supporting Fee Waiver (Income/Expense declaration).
8. **Form 07:** Proposed Order Granting Waiver of Court Filing Fees & Costs.
9. **Form 08:** Proposed Order Granting Expungement and Directing Sealing of Criminal Records.

## 4. Step-by-Step Filing Procedures

### Method A: Electronic Filing (IEFS / Odyssey File & Serve) — Preferred
1. Navigate to the Indiana E-Filing System portal at [https://courts.in.gov/efile](https://courts.in.gov/efile) (or your chosen certified E-Filing Service Provider, such as Odyssey File & Serve).
2. Create or sign into your Pro Se (self-represented) or Attorney account.
3. Select **"Start a New Case"**.
4. Choose the appropriate County (e.g., `Marion County - Circuit and Superior Courts`).
5. Select Case Category: **Civil (XP - Expungement)**.
6. Upload Form 01 as the **Lead Document** (`Petition for Expungement`).
7. Upload Forms 02 through 08 as **Attachments** or secondary documents.
8. If seeking a filing fee waiver, designate Form 05/06 as a Fee Waiver request.
9. Select E-Service for the Prosecuting Attorney and serve other statewide entities by Certified Mail.

### Method B: In-Person / Paper Filing with County Clerk
1. Print the complete generated PDF packet (single-sided on standard 20 lb white 8.5 × 11 in paper).
2. **Ink Signatures:** Sign Form 00, Form 01, Form 02, Form 04, Form 05, and Form 06 in blue or black ink. Form 02 and Form 06 require notarization before a Notary Public.
3. Make **5 copies** of the entire signed packet:
   - Copy 1: Court Clerk (original)
   - Copy 2: County Prosecuting Attorney
   - Copy 3: County Sheriff
   - Copy 4: Indiana State Police (Expungement Section)
   - Copy 5: Personal file copy (have the clerk file-stamp this copy for your records)
4. Deliver to the Office of the County Clerk in the county where the conviction occurred.

## 5. Mandatory Statutory Service Requirements

Under IC § 35-38-9-8(e), a copy of the petition must be served on:
1. **The Prosecuting Attorney** of the county where the petition is filed (via IEFS or Certified Mail).
2. **The County Sheriff** of the county where the charges originated.
3. **Indiana State Police (ISP):**  
   *Criminal History Repository / Expungement Section*  
   Indiana Government Center North

4. **Indiana Bureau of Motor Vehicles (BMV):** *(If offenses involve driver's license points or suspensions)*  
   *Legal Department / Records Division*  
   Indiana Government Center North  
   100 N. Senate Ave., Room N400  
   Indianapolis, IN 46204

## 6. Post-Filing Procedure & Timeline

- **Prosecutor's 30-Day Response Window:** The Prosecuting Attorney has 30 days after service to file an Answer, Objection, or Notice of No Objection.
- **Hearing Schedule:** 
  - For mandatory expungements (IC § 35-38-9-1, -2, -3) without prosecutor objection, courts typically grant relief without an in-person hearing.
  - For discretionary petitions (IC § 35-38-9-4, -5) or contested petitions, the court will set an evidentiary hearing date.
- **Distribution of Final Order:** Once signed by the Judge, the Clerk distributes certified copies of Form 08 to the Indiana Supreme Court Office of Judicial Administration (IOJA), ISP, BMV, and local law enforcement repositories to seal Odyssey records within 30 days.
